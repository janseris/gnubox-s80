# gnubox icon (Series 80)

For gnubox, the internet-over-Bluetooth sharing service.

A small 3D scene, rendered from above and to the side (`make_gnubox_icon.py` builds the devices
as boxes, turns the lids open about their hinges, lights each face by its angle with a
highlight, and maps the screens and keys onto the faces in perspective):

- A laptop behind on the left: its deck with keys and a touchpad, the lid open with a 16:10
  glass screen.
- The Nokia 9300 in front, open at about 110 degrees as in the photos: light silver, the wide
  screen showing the Desk (light lavender, yellow folder icons), four soft keys beside the
  screen, white keys and the round joystick on the keyboard half.
- A phone of today on the right, standing and leaning back a little: about 2:1, a 20:9 glass
  screen.
- Glowing orange radio waves (shaded as tubes) rising from the Nokia: the link it shares.

It's original artwork, generated by the script (Python 3, numpy, Pillow), and doesn't use the
Bluetooth logo, because it's a trademark.

| File | Size |
|---|---|
| `icon64.bmp`, `icon64_mask.bmp` | 64 x 50 (the big Desk icon) |
| `icon25.bmp`, `icon25_mask.bmp` | 25 x 20 (lists, the task switcher) |
| `preview.png` | both sizes, enlarged, on light and dark |

The 25x20 version has two wider arcs and no keys or screen details.

## Using it in an app

Copy the four BMPs into the app's `aif` folder and name them in the `.mmp`, colour icon then
mask, small size first:

```
AIF  gnubox.aif ..\aif gnuboxaif.rss c16 icon25.bmp icon25_mask.bmp icon64.bmp icon64_mask.bmp
```

with `num_icons = 2;` in the AIF resource. The masks are **black where the icon is drawn**,
white around it: the 9300 draws a masked bitmap where the mask is black. The masks are 1-bit,
so the edge pixels are blended toward the Desk's light background. The Desk may keep an app's
old icon until the phone restarts.
